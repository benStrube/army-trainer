## Not done / known issues
- **Readability is the noisy check.** Mean grade is **10.8** over 142 statements (target ~8th). 48 statements are over grade 12 and the deck-level warning fires. Doctrine vocabulary drives much of it. Only Opus can fix this by rewording the spec (which voids the review). It belongs on the WP 4.4 fix list.
- **Coverage is low by design:** 56 of 524 mandatory requirements (11%) are cited. Two chapters warn: ch-3 (9 of 110) and ch-6 (3 of 102). A 37-slide deck summarizes; whether that is enough is a WP 4.4 judgment. The full uncited list is in `qa.json` under `coverage.uncited`.
- Thresholds (`GRADE_WARN` 12, `GRADE_DECK` 9, `COVERAGE_WARN` 10%) are my calibration, not a decision from the scope doc. Adjust in `rules.py`.
- The date check only knows month-name, `d/m/y` and four-digit year forms; "May" counts only with a day or year.
- The footer-count check is a loose heuristic.
