## What the dry run taught (already folded in)
- My own slide title "Field artillery limits you **must** know" added a requirement, and titles weren't checked. **The check now fails on will/must/shall in titles.**
- Acronyms on slides weren't checked against the acronyms slide. **They are now (warning).** Publication designators like "FM 3-09" are exempt. The WP 2.1 fixture was missing FDC, BN, DFSCOORD, DIVARTY and FAB; I added them.
- The fixture's danger-close card said "where a weapon **will** hit". The glossary doesn't say that, so it was an added `will`. Reworded.
- "Read chapter 2" raised a number warning. References like chapter/table/para N are now ignored.
- ch-2 got 6 content slides in the dry run against a budget of 2. **In WP 2.3, prune to the budget:** keep the roles, FIST and target-vs-suspected-target slides. Fold the three-parts comparison into the divider blurb. Drop or move the weapons table.
