## Done
- ✅ **D15** in `docs/decisions/readability.md` (also in `docs/PROJECT_SCOPE.md` §0; §8.4 now points to it). The §8 reading level is measured **per slide**:
  - **Score:** the mean Flesch-Kincaid grade of the slide's on-slide statements.
  - **Statements:** each text field is one statement; fields under 8 words are skipped.
  - **Excluded:** speaker notes, and the title, acronyms and closing slides.
  - **Neutralized:** the publication's glossary terms, acronym meanings and abbreviations count as one word ("term").
  - **Pass:** at least 90% of scored slides at grade 9.0 or below.
  - Raw grades are reported alongside.
- ✅ **Reference values** on today's spec:
