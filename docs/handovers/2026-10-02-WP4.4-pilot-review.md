## Findings

### Content (Opus)
1. **Reading level is the biggest gap.** About a quarter of it is doctrinal vocabulary, which FK punishes but Soldiers must learn ("fire support coordination measure"). The rest is sentence length and compound items.
   - Worst slides: s29 (ch-5 doctrine), s14 Assess, s17 essential-task parts, s23/s24 table rows, s31 defense column, and the divider blurbs s12, s22 and s30.
2. **Coverage is low by design but has real gaps.**
   - 56 of 524 mandatory requirements are cited (11%). That number overstates the problem: in the 40-sentence sample of uncited requirements, only about 4–7 (10–17%) are things a junior Soldier acts on. The rest is staff and commander doctrine.
   - Uncited Soldier items worth a slide line:
     - "The observer must know where all friendly troops are to avoid endangering them" (para D-81, safety)
     - load COMSEC into GPS (C-9)
     - voice fire commands to the howitzers when digital fails (C-67)
     - computation of firing data must be accurate for all systems (C-63)
3. **Slides that spend budget on low-value content:**
   - s07 "Who trains you": training doctrine, not fires.
   - s29 competition/crisis: theater-level.
   - s31 defense column: staff planning.
   - s21 air support request flow: staff.
   - Each could give way to item 2's Soldier tasks.
4. **Safety framing.**
   - s27's sun-filter text is a CAUTION in the FM but isn't marked as one. The spec has no way to say so.
   - s13 shows danger-close distances without the FM's "don't confuse with minimum safe distances", which is only in the notes. The big_numbers pattern has no field for a one-line caveat.

### Rendering (Sonnet)
5. **s13 big numbers:** values wrap ("600 / m"). A value must stay on one line.
6. **Uneven text sizes between sibling boxes on one slide.** Each box auto-fits on its own: s09 FO card, s16 items 1 and 6, s18 last cell, s25 Voice column, s29, s35. Siblings should share one size, the smallest that fits.
7. **Dense tables shrink too far.** s33 (5 × 4 with long cells) renders at about 12 pt and is unreadable when projected. Split by rows or enforce a floor.
8. **Wasted space:**
   - The title slide's lower half is empty.
   - s11's two single-point columns leave large blank panels with the text low.

   Size panels to their content or top-align them.
9. **Tooling:** thumbnails need `libreoffice-impress`. Document it in the setup notes and CI, or detect it and say so loudly.

### Conversion (Opus, WP 1.2 owner)
10. **The 7 damaged tables** and run-together words ("highexplosive", "friendlyzone", "JP 3- 09"). Criterion 1 says "all tables intact". These also forced fidelity workarounds (WP 2.3 / 4.1).

## Recommendation on open question Q1 (per-chapter decks)
**Keep one deck per publication (D2). Don't add per-chapter decks for FMs.**
- In FM 3-09 the content the 25–40 budget leaves out is mostly staff and commander doctrine, the wrong audience for a junior-Soldier deck. Per-chapter decks would multiply slides about boards and command posts, not Soldier tasks.
- The Soldier-actionable gap (an estimated 50–80 requirement sentences) is closable inside one deck. Swap low-value slides (5.1c) and have the packet flag Soldier-actionable requirements. That flag is a small Opus packet change; fold it into 5.1c.
- **Revisit for ARs.** Allow an optional companion deck for a chapter only when it has its own audience (e.g. AR 350-1's unit-training chapters) **and** more Soldier-actionable requirements than one deck can hold. Decide that on the first AR pilot, not now.
- Related: 37 slides don't fit a 15-minute walkthrough. Instead of more decks, mark a ~12-slide **core path** for short sessions. The usability check uses one. A `core: true` slide flag could make it formal later (optional schema change, Opus).
