## Decisions made
- The spec holds no layout or colors and no source text. Speaker notes get verbatim text from the cited nodes at render time.
- The schema enforces hard limits (3–60 slides). The 25–40 budget, the ≥ 60% visual target and the template order are soft checks for `plan --check` (WP 2.2).
- `VISUAL_PATTERNS` defines what counts toward the ≥ 60% visual target. `checklist`, `takeaways` and `whats_new` count as lists.
