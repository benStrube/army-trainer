## Not done / known issues
- **22 "may be tight" notices** printed by `render` are on process flows, comparisons and cycles. They hit the minimum font size (as low as 14–16 pt) and the estimator says they might overflow. By eye they fit. I left them alone because splitting a process flow would change its meaning. If the user wants larger text, those slides need less text in the spec (Opus-owned).
- Text on the densest slides (tables, decision tree, role cards) is below the 18 pt body target.
- Decision trees use straight connectors; the cycle is a ring plus a legend. Both fine but plain (WP 5.2 can polish).
- The title slide has a lot of empty white space under the title band.
- `qa` and `build` are still stubs.

## Decisions made
- None new for the project. Renderer conventions: footer cites come from the tree's own `cite` strings; splitting is only for list-like patterns.
