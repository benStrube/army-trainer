  - The decision tree is a yes/no ladder.
  - Dense tables (s33 terrain table, s24 support relationships) are at 24 px and may run tight.
  - Nothing was checked visually (the type's instructions say not to verify unless asked).
- Footers are cut off at about 95 characters when a slide cites many nodes. The real renderer should use short cites, e.g. "paras 2-17–2-20".
